### 1\. Collection Schemas

This is the structure of your five MongoDB collections, based on the Mongoose models we finalized.

#### `users`

  * **email**: `String` (Required, Unique, Lowercase)
  * **password**: `String` (Required, Minlength 6, Hashed)
  * **role**: `String` (Required, Enum: `['employee', 'hr']`)
  * **employee**: `ObjectId` (Required, Unique, Ref: `Employee`)
  * **timestamps**: `true`

#### `employee_details`

  * **employeeId**: `String` (Required, Unique)
  * **firstName**: `String` (Required)
  * **lastName**: `String` (Required)
  * **personalEmail**: `String` (Required)
  * **designation**: `String` (Required)
  * **department**: `String`
  * **joiningDate**: `Date` (Required)
  * **dob**: `Date`
  * **phone**: `String`
  * **address**: `Object`
      * street: `String`
      * city: `String`
      * state: `String`
      * zip: `String`
  * **bankDetails**: `Object`
      * bankName: `String`
      * accountNumber: `String`
      * ifscCode: `String`
  * **taxInfo**: `Object`
      * pan: `String`
      * uan: `String`
  * **isActive**: `Boolean` (Default: `true`)
  * **timestamps**: `true`

#### `salary_details`

  * **employee**: `ObjectId` (Required, Unique, Ref: `Employee`)
  * **annualCTC**: `Number` (Required)
  * **effectiveDate**: `Date` (Default: `Date.now`)
  * **earnings**: `Array` of `Objects`
      * name: `String` (Required)
      * amount: `Number` (Required)
  * **deductions**: `Array` of `Objects`
      * name: `String` (Required)
      * amount: `Number`
      * isPercent: `Boolean` (Default: `false`)
      * percentOf: `String` (Default: `'Basic'`)
  * **employerContributions**: `Array` of `Objects`
      * name: `String` (Required)
      * amount: `Number`
      * isPercent: `Boolean` (Default: `false`)
      * percentOf: `String` (Default: `'Basic'`)
  * **timestamps**: `true`

#### `attendance_details`

  * **employee**: `ObjectId` (Required, Ref: `Employee`)
  * **month**: `Number` (Required, Min: 1, Max: 12)
  * **year**: `Number` (Required)
  * **totalWorkingDays**: `Number` (Required)
  * **daysPresent**: `Number` (Required)
  * **leaveWithoutPay**: `Number` (Default: 0)
  * **overtimeHours**: `Number` (Default: 0)
  * **timestamps**: `true`
  * **Index**: Unique compound index on `(employee, month, year)`

#### `payslips`

  * **employee**: `ObjectId` (Required, Ref: `Employee`)
  * **month**: `Number` (Required)
  * **year**: `Number` (Required)
  * **generatedOn**: `Date` (Default: `Date.now`)
  * **payrollInfo**: `Object`
      * totalWorkingDays: `Number` (Required)
      * daysPaid: `Number` (Required)
      * lopDays: `Number` (Default: 0)
  * **earnings**: `Array` of `Objects`
      * name: `String` (Required)
      * amount: `Number` (Required)
      * type: `String` (Enum: `['fixed', 'variable', 'reimbursement']`)
  * **deductions**: `Array` of `Objects`
      * name: `String` (Required)
      * amount: `Number` (Required)
      * type: `String` (Enum: `['statutory', 'tax', 'lop', 'other']`)
  * **grossEarnings**: `Number` (Required)
  * **totalDeductions**: `Number` (Required)
  * **netPay**: `Number` (Required)
  * **status**: `String` (Enum: `['pending', 'paid', 'generated']`, Default: `generated`)
  * **paymentDate**: `Date`
  * **timestamps**: `true`
  * **Index**: Unique compound index on `(employee, month, year)`
